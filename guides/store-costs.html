The Real Cost of Running an Online Store

A research-based guide to the real cost of running an online store. Use this checklist to compare real options and verify details before making a financial decision.

Fixed costs

List platform or hosting charges, domains, email, essential apps and recurring services. Avoid assuming free trials continue indefinitely.

Variable costs

Include product cost, payment fees, packing, delivery, returns, chargebacks and customer support per order.

Contribution margin

Subtract variable costs from sales revenue. The remainder must cover fixed expenses, customer acquisition and taxes before profit exists.

Cash flow

Suppliers may require payment before processors release funds. Plan for reserves, refunds and seasonal demand.

Scenario testing

Model low, medium and high sales volumes using conservative assumptions. Check current provider pricing before publishing specific figures.

Action checklist

  1. Write down your actual requirements and constraints.
  2. Verify current specifications, terms and costs with the provider.
  3. Compare at least two realistic alternatives.
  4. Document any unresolved questions before committing money.
